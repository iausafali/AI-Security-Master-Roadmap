Terminal Exit Sequence
1. Derive: Reconstruct the underlying math/logic from first principles, unaided.
2. Implement: Build it from scratch - no wrapping an existing library and calling it done.
3. Benchmark: Compare against a real, independent standard (NumPy, scikit-learn, a published CVE, an undefended baseline) with a stated numeric bar.
4. Break: Attack or stress your own implementation until it fails.
5. Patch: Fix the break, then re-attack to confirm the fix actually holds.
6. Document: A written artifact another engineer could audit without asking you questions.
